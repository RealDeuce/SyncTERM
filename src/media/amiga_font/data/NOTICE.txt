Native Amiga bitmap font data

Workbench 1.3 and Workbench 2.x+ files originate from Commodore-Amiga's
native bitmap font sets. The additional native Courier/30, Courier/36,
Times/30 and Times/36 files identify themselves as AWeb 39.0, dated 1996.
The native Pearl/8 file comes from the local Amiga font drawer.

The original files are retained unchanged. Their individual SHA-256
identities and source paths are recorded in manifest.json; disk-image
identities are recorded in provenance.json.
